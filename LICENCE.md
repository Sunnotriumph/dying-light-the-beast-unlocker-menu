## 🧩 About

**Dying Light The Beast Companion** is a curated list of Dying Light The Beast open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, survival tracker, compass overlay
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — inventory catalog, blueprint manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — parkour layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Survival Helpers
- Compass Overlay — waypoint and objective tracker
- Stamina Notes — visual meter for parkour study
- Loot Tracker — highlight crafting materials and resources
- Playback Speed — adjust movement study pace (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Blueprint Catalog — browse craftable items and mods
- Custom Outfit Notes — organize player cosmetics
- Night Vision Notes — improved visibility in dark zones

### 🏋️ Practice Layout
- Practice Mode Notes — segment study on any layout
- Climb Control — manually toggle climb assist mid-run
- Jump Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the editor
